What we build for government

Five capability areas spanning digital public service — from the citizen's first tap to the department's last approval.

Citizen Service Portals

Unified, mobile-first portals for citizens to discover, apply for and track public services — accessible, multilingual and self-service by design.

  • Single sign-on & unified citizen profile
  • Application, tracking & status workflows
  • Multilingual & accessibility-compliant UX
  • Payment gateway & fee-collection integration

E-Governance Platforms

End-to-end digital workflows for departments — file movement, approvals, scheme management — replacing paper trails with auditable digital records.

  • Digital file & workflow management
  • Scheme & beneficiary management
  • Role-based approvals & audit trails
  • Dashboards for administrators & officers

Digital Public Infrastructure

Interoperable, API-first building blocks — identity, registries, consent and data exchange — built to plug into national and state DPI stacks.

  • API-first, interoperable architecture
  • Registry & master-data platforms
  • Consent & data-exchange layers
  • Population-scale performance engineering

GovTech AI

Applied AI where it moves the needle — document intelligence at scale, and grievance systems that route and resolve citizen complaints faster.

  • Document intelligence & OCR at scale
  • AI-assisted grievance routing & triage
  • Conversational assistants for citizen queries
  • Predictive analytics for planning & delivery

Data Security & Compliance

Security engineered into every layer, aligned to the standards government procurement demands — ISO 27001 practices and India's DPDP Act.

  • ISO 27001-aligned security practices
  • DPDP Act 2023 compliance by design
  • Data residency & encryption controls
  • VAPT, secure SDLC & audit readiness
Why Globalion

A Pune-based, AI-first partner built for government mandates.

Government projects reward partners who understand the mandate, not just the code — data residency, procurement standards, accessibility and long-term maintainability. Globalion is an Indian company headquartered in Pune, engineered for those realities from the start.

Being AI-first means applying document intelligence, conversational assistants and predictive analytics as production systems that hold up under audit and load — not buzzwords.

Building in India, for India, keeps sensitive citizen data within jurisdiction, aligned to the DPDP Act 2023 and ISO 27001 practices, with a team available in person for the long horizons public programs demand.
