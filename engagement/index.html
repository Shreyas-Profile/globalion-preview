Engagement Models

Work with us the way that fits your goal.

Four proven engagement models — a full team, one specialist, or a fixed outcome — so structure never gets in the way of momentum.

01

Dedicated Development Teams

A cross-functional team — engineers, QA, DevOps and a lead — working as a dedicated extension of your organisation.

When to choose it: Best for an evolving roadmap where you want a stable team that builds domain knowledge over time.

What's included
  • Handpicked, ring-fenced team
  • Dedicated tech lead & delivery manager
  • Your tools, sprints & ceremonies
  • Scale team size up or down as needed
02

Staff Augmentation

Senior specialists who slot into your existing team and report through your management, filling specific skill or capacity gaps.

When to choose it: Best when your team is strong but stretched, or missing a niche skill — AI/ML, cloud, mobile — for a defined period.

What's included
  • Vetted specialists on demand
  • Integrated into your workflow
  • Flexible ramp-up & ramp-down
  • Rapid onboarding, minimal overhead
03

Fixed-Scope Project Delivery

We own an outcome end-to-end against an agreed scope, timeline and budget — from discovery through build, testing and handover.

When to choose it: Best when requirements are well defined and you want predictable cost with single-point accountability.

What's included
  • Fixed scope, timeline & budget
  • Discovery & solution architecture
  • Milestone-based delivery & UAT
  • Documentation & knowledge transfer
04

Managed Services / RPO

Ongoing ownership of the run — application support, infrastructure and maintenance — or Recruitment Process Outsourcing for tech hiring.

When to choose it: Best when you need reliable operations beyond the build, or a partner to source and screen technology talent at scale.

What's included
  • Application support & maintenance
  • Infrastructure & cloud operations
  • SLA-backed service levels
  • Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)
