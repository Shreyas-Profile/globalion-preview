Overview

The hiring process is broken in both directions — candidates struggle with preparation and self-assessment while recruiters struggle with consistency, bias, and throughput. InterviewPanda is Globalion's AI-powered interview intelligence platform that improves outcomes at both ends of the hiring funnel simultaneously. For candidates, it provides personalized, interactive mock interview practice with AI coaching and skill gap feedback. For recruiters and HR teams, it provides structured, AI-assisted evaluation and candidate intelligence dashboards that make hiring decisions faster, fairer, and better documented.

Capabilities

Everything InterviewPanda does, built to production standard.

Personalized Interview Coaching

AI-driven coaching plans built from your target role, current skill level, and past performance — not generic advice.

AI Mock Interviews

Live AI-conducted mock interviews with natural conversation, real-time feedback on content quality, communication, and confidence.

Skill Gap Analysis

Precise identification of skill gaps relative to role requirements, with curated learning resources and practice priorities.

Industry Question Banks

Curated, role-specific question banks covering technical, behavioral, and case interview formats across 50+ industries and job functions.

Recruiter Analytics Dashboard

Interview analytics, candidate comparison tools, and structured evaluation workflows for HR teams conducting high-volume hiring.

Candidate Scoring

Objective, multi-dimensional candidate scoring on technical knowledge, communication, problem-solving, and cultural fit indicators.

Video Interview Analysis

Optional AI analysis of practice interview recordings for delivery, clarity, pacing, and non-verbal communication coaching.

Institution Analytics

Campus placement cell dashboard tracking student readiness scores, placement outcomes, and employer feedback at the cohort level.
