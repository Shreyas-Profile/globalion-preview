Overview

Compliance AI Pitch is a dedicated deployment of the cmplihr.ai platform reserved for sales, partnership and analyst conversations. It runs on the same production codebase as cmplihr.ai with an isolated database so demo data never mixes with real customer records. Use it to walk prospects through the full compliance journey — from onboarding, workforce capture and gap analysis to AI-generated evidence checklists — without any risk of exposing another client's data.

Capabilities

Everything Compliance AI Pitch does, built to production standard.

Same codebase as production cmplihr.ai

Every feature demoable here is what ships to real customers — nothing is mocked or hard-coded for the pitch.

Isolated demo data

Own database (`compliance_pitch`) so scripted demo organisations, users and evidence never mix with real customer records.

Reset-friendly

Data can be wiped and reseeded without touching cmplihr.ai production — safe for parallel demos.

Full AI pipeline

OpenRouter, gap analyser, evidence-suggest and additional-questions endpoints all wired end-to-end.
