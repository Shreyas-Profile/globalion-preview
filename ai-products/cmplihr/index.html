Overview

Labour law compliance in India is a moving target — with over 40 central and 100+ state-level statutes, constantly updated notifications, and significant penalty exposure for violations. cmplihr.ai is Globalion's AI-powered compliance intelligence platform that transforms compliance from a manual, reactive function into an automated, proactive, and fully auditable capability. The platform continuously monitors regulatory changes, maps them to your specific workforce profile and operating jurisdictions, and surfaces actionable alerts before exposure becomes liability.

Capabilities

Everything cmplihr.ai does, built to production standard.

Automated Compliance Monitoring

Real-time tracking of labour law amendments across all 28 states and Union Territories, mapped to your workforce jurisdictions.

Multi-State Coverage

Comprehensive coverage of central and state-level labour laws including Shops & Establishments, PF, ESI, Professional Tax, Gratuity, and more.

Payroll Compliance Audit

AI-powered audit of payroll computations against statutory requirements, flagging deviations before they become penalties.

Contractor & Vendor Compliance

End-to-end compliance tracking for contract labour — principal employer liability, Form submission tracking, and contractor audit trails.

AI Legal Interpretation

Natural language query interface that answers complex compliance questions with citations from applicable statutes and circulars.

Compliance Calendar

Jurisdiction-specific compliance calendar with automated reminders for return filing deadlines, license renewals, and periodic obligations.

Penalty Risk Alerts

Proactive risk scoring that identifies high-exposure compliance gaps before they become inspector visits or litigation.

Audit-Ready Reports

One-click generation of compliance certificates, registers, and reports formatted for labour department submissions and internal audits.
