Overview

In an environment where public opinion shifts in hours and a single viral narrative can reshape perception, organizations can no longer afford to listen only when a crisis has already landed. Social Listening AI is Globalion's public intelligence platform that ingests, analyzes, and synthesizes public conversations from social media, news, forums, and regional digital channels in real time — turning unstructured opinion data into structured, decision-ready intelligence for communications, strategy, and risk management teams.

Capabilities

Everything Social Listening AI does, built to production standard.

Multi-Platform Data Ingestion

Aggregates data from Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, regional news, and web forums at scale.

AI Sentiment Analysis

Fine-tuned NLP models trained on Indian political and social contexts provide accurate multi-class sentiment beyond positive/negative.

Topic Clustering

Unsupervised clustering automatically identifies emerging narratives and issue clusters without requiring manual keyword configuration.

Influencer & Source Tracking

Identify the key accounts, publications, and voices driving specific narratives, ranked by reach, engagement, and credibility scores.

Crisis Detection Alerts

Velocity-based anomaly detection identifies sudden sentiment shifts or narrative surges and notifies stakeholders before they escalate.

Geographic Sentiment Mapping

District and constituency-level sentiment heat maps for political and governance use cases across Indian states.

Trend Prediction

Time-series forecasting models predict the likely trajectory of current issues, giving communications teams a preparation window.

Custom Dashboards

Role-based dashboards for C-suite, communications teams, and field teams with configurable KPIs and one-click report exports.
